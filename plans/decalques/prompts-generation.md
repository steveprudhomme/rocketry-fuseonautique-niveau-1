## Proposition 1

Use case: product-mockup. Create one polished original graphic-design proposal board for a Quebec hobby rocket named exactly "Chasse Galerie 1". Large landscape board on warm off-white background, restrained editorial typography. Left third: tall flat rectangular full-tube wrap artwork, width:height about 1:2.7, NOT a sticker sheet. Right two thirds: two full-length clean studio mockups of the SAME decorated rocket seen from different rotational angles, vertical nose upwards, both entirely in frame. Rocket shape: ordinary LOC-IV style hobby rocket, slender cylindrical tube about 8.5 tube diameters long with a rounded elongated ellipsoidal nose about 3.25 diameters long; THREE swept clipped plywood fins near base, radial 120 degrees, not missiles, no boosters. Thin separation seam between upper removable tube and lower tube; printed design must be visually continuous but film does not bridge seam. Navy painted nose and blue fins complementary, artwork on cylindrical body. Do not depict launch, fire, machinery or internal cutaway. Legible exact name "Chasse Galerie 1" running along the upper-middle tube, integrated in flat artwork and mockups. Quebec flag: one faithful small blue rectangular flag, centered white upright cross dividing FOUR blue quadrants each containing ONE upright white fleur-de-lys, all pointing UP, four total; no maple leaves, no Union Jack, no invented flags. On the wrap show recognizable original chasse-galerie flying canoe with voyageurs and paddles soaring above a Quebec conifer forest. Keep flag and lettering away from seams and fins. Design board is a concept, NOT a dimensioned fabrication plan. Small footer exactly "Concept visuel — dimensions à valider". No measurement labels, no extra slogans, no other logos. Very high visual polish, crisp motifs.  Board title exactly "01 — La traversée nocturne". Art direction: painterly illustrated nocturnal panorama, deep midnight navy and luminous Quebec blue, subtle cyan mist, restrained white starlight. A recognizable wooden flying canoe with voyageurs sweeps diagonally through the middle of the body, over layered fir forest silhouettes at bottom. Generous quiet navy area behind elegant white name. Delicate atmosphere but strong readable silhouette. Satiny surface, not shiny. Heroic folklore, no horror imagery.
